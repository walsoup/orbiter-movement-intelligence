What this is. Orbiter watches an exercise session through one ordinary camera and measures how the movement changes over time: repetitions, joint range of motion, left/right symmetry, tempo and movement energy. On a long mission, that trend — measured the same way every week — is an early signal that a crew member's exercise countermeasures are, or are not, holding up. It needs no wearables. Pose tracking runs in the browser; optional AI review requires an internet connection and sends summary metrics: Google's MediaPipe Pose Landmarker (33 body landmarks) runs entirely in this browser, and every number on this page is derived live from joint geometry.

Honest limits. This is a change-spotting tool, not a clinical measurement. Scores are only comparable between your own sessions recorded under the same setup. Sessions under 15 seconds, or with the body tracked less than half the time, are reported but not scored.
